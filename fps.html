🌑 Rustbelt Moon — first-person prototype

This is an early tech test for turning SPAC3's minigame worlds into first-person 3D — same map layout as the regular Mini Space Crafts game, but walkable in first person with your weapon on screen. Fight the same Scrap Crawlers and Junk Golems as the 2D game for real scrap, using whatever weapon you have equipped. Still single-player here -- no other players visible yet.

Click anywhere to start. Arrow keys to move, mouse or WASD to look around, left-click or Space to attack, Esc to release the mouse.
